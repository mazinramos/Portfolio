About Me

I'm a Computer Science student and web developer. I turn coursework and side ideas into real, working products — from small business sites to full systems with dashboards and databases behind them.

I like problems that have a clear before-and-after: a clinic that was tracking patients on paper, a store that needed an actual checkout flow, a student who needed a resume in ten minutes instead of two hours. My approach is simple — understand what you actually need, build it properly, and keep it easy to maintain afterward.
